## Audience

The owner or marketing lead at an industrial manufacturer. Smart, busy, not
an SEO. They have never heard of a "grounding query" and should not need to.
Page one answers: are we showing up when AI answers questions in our market,
and is it improving?

## Sections, in order

1. **Where you stand.** Three numbers, each naming its source and period:
   share of AI retrievals in your topics that cited you (Bing); how many
   tracked buyer questions name you, out of the total, and on which engines;
   sessions that arrived from an AI assistant (Analytics). Two sentences on
   what they mean together. If one has no data, say so rather than drop it.
2. **What buyers ask, and whether you come up.** Group the phrases AI uses to
   find companies like this into topics; give each a citation rate and the
   volume behind it. Strongest first, then the gaps worth closing. Explain
   once that these are phrases the AI generates when hunting for sources, not
   words a customer typed. Treat the company's own name separately: below
   about 90% there is a problem worth naming.
3. **Who comes up instead.** Competitors named in the answers we collected,
   how often, and for which questions. No data yet means tracking needs to
   run longer, not that the field is empty.
4. **The pages AI relies on.** Pages that get cited, and pages assistants
   send visitors to. Name the strongest two or three. A page cited but not
   visited is normal now, not a fault.
5. **What changed.** Compare with the previous report. If this is the first,
   say so; never present a first snapshot as a trend.
6. **What we're doing next.** Three to five actions, each tied to a specific
   finding above. No generic advice.

## Tone

Plain and direct. No jargon without a plain-English gloss on first use; never
"GEO" or "AI SEO". Neither hype AI search nor dismiss it.

Report only measured data: Bing citations, Analytics sessions, and answers we
collected by asking the engines. Never include Search Console query-syntax
guesses — inference next to measurement makes the measurement less
believable. Name the source of every number. Say that assistants which strip
the referrer are counted as direct traffic, so AI sessions are a floor.
Missing values render as "—", never zero. No blended "AI visibility score":
it hides which input moved and cannot be acted on.

## Sign-off

A short paragraph from Peninsula Industries offering to walk through the
findings and answer anything unclear.
